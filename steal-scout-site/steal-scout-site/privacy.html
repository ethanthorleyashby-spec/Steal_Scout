Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Steal Scout collects, why, and who it's shared with. The short version: we collect what we need to run your membership and your searches, we don't sell your personal information, and you can ask us to delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account and billing details. Your name and email address when you join. Payment is handled by Stripe, which collects your card details directly. We see only limited information such as the card brand, last four digits, and payment status.
  • Waitlist emails. If you join the launch waitlist, we store the email address you enter.
  • Searches. The searches you run in Steal Scout and the results you click, so we can return results and improve rankings.
  • Basic usage data. Information your browser sends automatically, such as device type, browser, approximate location from your IP address, and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To run your membership, process payments, and send account emails, including the email before your first charge.
  • To run your searches and rank results.
  • To tell waitlist members when Steal Scout launches.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse, and improve the service.

We'll only send marketing emails if you've joined the waitlist or become a member, and every one includes a way to unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-site advertising. We share information only with the service providers that help us run Steal Scout, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe, for payments and subscriptions.
  • Netlify, which hosts our website and stores waitlist sign-ups.
  • Product data providers, which receive the text of your searches (not your name or email) so they can return listings.
  • AI providers, which receive the text of your searches and the listings found (not your name or email) to calculate fit scores.
  • Email providers, to send account and launch emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or Steal Scout.

Links to stores

When you click through to a store, you leave Steal Scout and that store's privacy policy applies. Some links are affiliate links, and the store or affiliate network may set cookies to record that you came from Steal Scout.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your membership is active and for a limited time afterward for billing, tax, and legal records. You can ask us to delete your search history at any time. Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@YOURDOMAIN.com. We'll respond within 30 days. Some records, such as payment history, may need to be kept for legal reasons.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit who can access your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we never store full card numbers ourselves.

Children

Steal Scout isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and email members before the change takes effect.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@YOURDOMAIN.com.
